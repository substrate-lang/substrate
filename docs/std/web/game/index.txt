;; std/web/game
;; The game loop: what turns three functions into a game in a web page.

std/web/game/draw     Drawing for games: sprites, rectangles and text, on the 2D canvas or the GPU.
std/web/game/format   Formatting text, for games: numbers and strings put into text by a pattern.
std/web/game/game     The game loop: what turns three functions into a game in a web page.
std/web/game/input    Keyboard and mouse state for games.
std/web/game/movers   Movers: things that move over the land among the things that stand on it.
std/web/game/noise    Noise for making worlds.
std/web/game/props    Props: the things that stand in a world and do not move.
std/web/game/sound    Sound for games: sound files, and short synthesized tones.
std/web/game/space    Space: a grid of cells over the world, for what is near, in the way, or hit by a line.
std/web/game/store    A store: objects of one type that come and go, remembered by handles.
std/web/game/terrain  Land as a height map: meshed for the scene, asked for its height and slope, walked on.
std/web/game/tiles    Tile maps for 2D games: levels of square tiles, bodies that move through them, a camera.
std/web/game/timers   Timers for games: things that happen later.
std/web/game/trap     Stopping the program when it finds a mistake in itself.
std/web/game/vector   A vector: a row of items of one type, numbered from 0, that grows as items are added.
std/web/game/voxels   Worlds made of blocks: a grid, bodies that move through it, rays, and its meshes.
