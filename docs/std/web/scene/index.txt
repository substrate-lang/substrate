;; std/web/scene
;; A 3D scene drawn by the GPU: meshes lit by a sun, under a sky, made into a picture.

std/web/scene/camera     The camera of a 3D scene: where it is, how it is turned, and what it sees.
std/web/scene/light      The light of a 3D scene: the sun, the ambient light, the sky, the fog and the shadows.
std/web/scene/materials  Materials painted by the GPU from noise, into one texture of many layers with mip levels.
std/web/scene/math       The mathematics a 3D game needs that wasm has no instructions for: trigonometry, matrices.
std/web/scene/meshes     Meshes drawn by the GPU: placed many times, with painted materials, lit by a sun, and water.
std/web/scene/models     Models and figures: things made of parts that move on joints.
std/web/scene/particles  Particles: smoke, fireflies and splashes, moved by a compute shader and drawn as soft discs.
std/web/scene/post       The picture pass: the textures a scene is drawn into, made into the picture on the screen.
std/web/scene/scene      A 3D scene drawn by the GPU: meshes lit by a sun, under a sky, made into a picture.
std/web/scene/shader     The shader form: a shader's bindings, buffers and stages written once, checked when compiled.
std/web/scene/sky        The sky: what a scene shows where there is nothing, drawn in one pass over the screen.
std/web/scene/sprites    Sprites drawn by the GPU: a frame's sprites gathered into a batch and drawn in a few commands.
