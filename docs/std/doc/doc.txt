;; std/doc/doc
;; A module's documentation from its source, read and never run: its header comment, its language and what it requires,
;; each named as the compiler finds it, what it exports, and its items. Its language's doc-forms say which forms define
;; what: its language form's module's, or that module's language's, and compiler/substrate.sub's for a module with none.
;; An item is every form that defines one name: its kind, from the earliest of its entries in doc-forms; each form as
;; written, less what doc-forms leave out, with the comment directly above it; the section it is in; and whether the
;; module exports it.
;;
;; In a pattern, NAME is the name a form defines, _ matches what a form's text leaves out, other symbols starting with a
;; capital match anything, X ... matches any number of X, a symbol after a dot matches a list's rest, and other symbols
;; match themselves. Kinds with a meaning here: within (the forms FORM matched are documented too), requires (MODULE
;; is required), exports (each EXPORT is exported), reexports (an export that exports another module's names) and
;; renamed (an export that exports NAME as AS).
;; requires compiler/macros compiler/roots compiler/reader host/fs std/package/path

(define (doc-header [d datum]) -> datum)

(define (doc-reexports [d datum]) -> datum)

(define (doc-items [d datum]) -> datum)
