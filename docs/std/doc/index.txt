;; std/doc
;; A module's documentation from its source, read and never run: its header comment, its language, what it requires
;; and exports, and its items. Its language's doc-forms say which forms define what (compiler/substrate.sub's describes
;; Substrate). An item is every form that defines one name: its kind, from the earliest of its entries in doc-forms;
;; each form as written, less what doc-forms leave out, with the comment directly above it; the section it is in; and
;; whether the module exports it.
;;
;; In a pattern, NAME is the name a form defines, _ matches what a form's text leaves out, other symbols starting with a
;; capital match anything, X ... matches any number of X, a symbol after a dot matches a list's rest, and other symbols
;; match themselves. Kinds with a meaning here: within (the forms FORM matched are documented too), requires (MODULE
;; is required), exports (each EXPORT is exported), reexports (an export that exports another module's names) and
;; renamed (an export that exports NAME as AS).

std/doc/doc   A module's documentation from its source, read and never run: its header comment, its language, what it requires and exports, and its items.
std/doc/html  Documentation as HTML pages, one per module and one per directory, at each one's name under the output directory: a module's page has its items under the module's sections, public then private, with a sidebar of them by kind; a directory's lists its modules and directories.
std/doc/text  Documentation as text: a module as its declarations would read in a header file, and a directory as a list of its modules.
