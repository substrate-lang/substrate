;; std

std/archive/
std/compress/
std/crypto/
std/doc/        A module's documentation from its source, read and never run: its header comment, its language and what it requires, each named as the compiler finds it, what it exports, and its items.
std/encoding/
std/html/       HTML: the elements and attributes of the standard, and writing it: an element's start tag is html-open!, its attributes, then html-opened!, and its end tag html-close!; text and attribute values are escaped.
std/http/       HTTP/1.1 messages, parsed and written.
std/languages/
std/log/        Logs after Go's log/slog: an entry is a level, a message and attributes, written as one line of key=value text or JSON when its level is at least the logger's.
std/meta/
std/package/
std/web/        The language of a web page's program: Substrate, ui.sub's views, and app: model, messages, update, view.
