;; std/compress

std/compress/deflate  DEFLATE compression (RFC 1951) as a stream, after zlib: a 32 KB window, hash chains of 3-byte strings, greedy matching at levels 1 to 3 and lazy at 4 to 9 with zlib's settings, and each block stored, fixed or with its own codes, whichever is shortest.
std/compress/gzip     The gzip format (RFC 1952) around DEFLATE data, and its CRC-32.
std/compress/inflate  DEFLATE decoding (RFC 1951) of whole buffers, with lookup tables as zlib and libdeflate decode.
