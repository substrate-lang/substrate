;; std/crypto

std/crypto/hmac      HMAC (RFC 2104) with SHA-256, and what is built on it: HKDF (RFC 5869), which derives keys from a secret, and PBKDF2 (RFC 8018), which derives them from a password.
std/crypto/password  Passwords stored as PHC strings of PBKDF2-SHA256, $pbkdf2-sha256$i=ITERATIONS$SALT$HASH in Base64 without padding, worked out in a worker.
std/crypto/sha1      SHA-1 (RFC 3174), over bytes added in pieces or given at once.
std/crypto/sha256    SHA-256 (FIPS 180-4), over bytes added in pieces or given at once.
